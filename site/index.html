PostHog logo, 3D printable

About 104 × 56 × 8 mm, split into one piece per color. It looks the same from both sides, and the pieces press together with built-in joints.

Download Bambu Studio project (.3mf)

One plate, printed by object, supports off, about 34 minutes on an X2D. Open it in Bambu Studio and sync it to your printer, or use the STLs.

Parts

  • Blue stripeBambu PLA Basic Cobalt Blue STL
  • Orange stripeBambu PLA Basic Orange STL
  • Yellow stripeBambu PLA Basic Sunflower Yellow STL
  • HeadBambu PLA Basic Black STL
  • EyeBambu PLA Basic Jade White STL

Assembly

  1. Each stripe has two tabs on its right edge. Slide them into the pockets on the next piece, from left to right.
  2. Crush ribs on the tabs make it a firm press fit. A drop of superglue in each pocket makes it permanent.
  3. Drop the eye into the head and glue it. It has a little clearance so it doesn't bind.
